# Data for TDM and AI: Access and Conditions

## Table of contents

- [Use online](#use-online)
- [Contents](#contents)
- [Files](#files)
- [Notes](#notes)
- [Licence](#licence)

An overview of interfaces, data formats, quotas and licence terms for text and data mining (TDM) and AI use of content licensed by the University Library Bern. It covers 20 publishers and 5 databases and metadata services.

## Use online

- [Deutsch](https://ub-unibe-ch.github.io/ds-pytools/tdm-ai/)
- [English](https://ub-unibe-ch.github.io/ds-pytools/tdm-ai/en.html)

## Contents

- **Overview table** with TDM and AI status, access route, formats and quotas per provider, filterable by access type and data type
- **Details per provider** with key links, API key requirements, available content and formats, and a summary of the licence terms
- Crossref coverage of TDM links and licence information per publisher (journal articles; "recent" = from 2024, "older" = up to 2023)

## Notes

The information is a summary for orientation only and is not legal advice. Licence agreements take precedence over information on provider websites. Contract terms for some providers are not public. Please contact [Digital Scholarship](https://www.ub.unibe.ch/services/digital_scholarship/index_eng.html) ([ds.ub@unibe.ch](mailto:ds.ub@unibe.ch)) before any TDM or AI use of licensed content.
